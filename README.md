# DEX Key Maker – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

DEX Key Maker, also marketed as Key Maker Dubai, is a Dubai-based locksmith business established in 2013. The company provides residential, automotive, and commercial locksmith services, including key duplication, lock repairs, smart lock installation, and emergency locksmith assistance.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting DEX Key Maker as a dependable, accessible locksmith service across Dubai, with clearer service categories, consistent business information, and prominent contact options.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent and Credible Business Information
- Standardised company information using verified details, including the 2013 establishment year.
- Removed inconsistent and unsupported business statistics.
- Improved the presentation of company background and business information to strengthen customer confidence.

### 2. Clearer Service Presentation and Correct Images
- Organised services into three distinct categories: residential, automotive, and commercial locksmith services.
- Improved service cards to help visitors identify the right solution more easily.
- Addressed the incorrect commercial service image by replacing the Getty Images webpage reference with a valid direct image URL.
- Ensured all three service images use appropriate image sources.

### 3. Consistent 24/7 Service Messaging
- Standardised the presentation of the company's advertised 24/7 locksmith availability.
- Removed conflicting response-time claims that could create unrealistic customer expectations.
- Focused on communicating continuous availability without promising unverified arrival times.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more organised and professional homepage appearance with clearer content hierarchy.
- **Layout:** Separated residential, automotive, and commercial services into easily identifiable sections.
- **User Experience:** Improved service discovery and made essential contact information easier to access.
- **Image Consistency:** Corrected the commercial service image source and addressed image-loading reliability across the three service categories.
- **Content Consistency:** Standardised company information and 24/7 availability messaging throughout the homepage.
- **Overall Structure:** Established a clearer presentation of business credibility, service offerings, and customer contact pathways.

The redesigned homepage provides DEX Key Maker with a more consistent, service-focused online presence while making it easier for Dubai customers to understand its locksmith services and find the appropriate assistance.
